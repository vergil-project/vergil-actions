
# Release 2.1.40 (2026-10-07)

## Bug fixes

- **enforce CI-evidence gate explicitly and give language-less quality gates real evidence (#926)**
The ci-quality common job now captures its vrg-validate --check common transcript as quality-common.log and uploads it as a quality partial, so a language-less repo's quality gate carries a real report instead of an evidence.json-only payload. cd-release follows the evidence gate and attach steps with explicit enforcement guards that fail the job on a failed outcome or a missing positive success output (passed/attached), so an enforcing failure can no longer be masked into a published release. Ref vergil-project/vergil-actions#925
