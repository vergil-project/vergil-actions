## CI

- **move the nightly ops cron to the 01:xx UTC hour (#1200)**
GitHub schedule events are landing 7 to 9 hours late, pushing the 06:45 UTC run into the US Eastern working morning. Moving the hour to 01 keeps the lagged run overnight. The minute (the per-repo stagger) is unchanged.
