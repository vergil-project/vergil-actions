
# Release 2.1.20 (2026-08-05)

## Bug fixes

- **skip pip's vendored SBOM in fs/image vuln scans (#798)**
* fix(trivy): skip pip's vendored SBOM in fs/image vuln scans

pip 26.x ships pip/_vendor/bom.cdx.json, a CycloneDX SBOM of pip's vendored dependencies. Trivy ingests it and reports those vendored packages (e.g. setuptools 70.3.0, msgpack 1.1.2) as findings even though they are pip internals, not the scan target's attack surface — breaking the docker-publish gate on every pip-bearing image fleet-wide.

Add --skip-files '**/pip/_vendor/bom.cdx.json' to the fs and image vuln scans so Trivy no longer parses that SBOM. The sbom generation mode is left untouched (an SBOM should describe everything present).

Verified against the real vergil-containers prod-base image on Trivy 0.71.0 (the action default) and 0.72.0: the skip clears the setuptools and msgpack false positives while every other finding still reports. Lets consumer repos drop their per-image rm workaround (vergil-containers#481).
